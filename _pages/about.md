Sezgin Ayabakan is an Associate Professor of Management Information Systems and Harold Schaefer Fellow at Temple University, Fox School of Business.

His research examines how artificial intelligence (AI) and digital technologies shape healthcare delivery, patient outcomes, clinical decision-making, care utilization, and organizational performance. His work has appeared in MIS Quarterly, Information Systems Research, Accounting, Organizations and Society, Journal of the Association for Information Systems, and Journal of the American Medical Informatics Association.

His research spans conversational AI in mental health screening, telehealth, electronic health records, healthcare financial technologies, medical AI devices, and value-based care. His chatbot research was cited in World Health Organization guidance on artificial intelligence for health, and his research on health information sharing was cited in federal rulemaking associated with the 21st Century Cures Act.

His current research focuses on emerging applications of AI in healthcare, including ambient AI in medical consultations, AI-enabled prescreening, medical diagnostic technologies, patient safety, and causal AI. Through this work, he seeks to understand when and how AI can improve healthcare—and how organizations can address the accompanying challenges involving trust, privacy, safety, and implementation.

Sezgin holds a Ph.D. in Management Science from the University of Texas at Dallas, an M.S. in Industrial and Systems Engineering from the University of Florida, and a B.S. in Industrial Engineering from Bilkent University.

Outside academia, he supports humanitarian and animal-welfare organizations.
